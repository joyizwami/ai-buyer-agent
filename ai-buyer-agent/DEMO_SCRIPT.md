# AI Buyer Agent Demo Script

## 1. Opening pitch (20-30 seconds)
"This project is an AI-powered purchasing assistant for teams and businesses. A user can describe what they want in natural language, and the system will find the best product, validate it against budget and approval policies, and create a payment flow with full transaction tracking."

## 2. Show the problem (30 seconds)
"In most companies, buying is still slow and manual. People message a manager, compare products manually, overlook spend rules, and end up with inconsistent approvals and weak audit trails. This system removes that friction."

## 3. Show product search and AI reasoning (60 seconds)
- type a request such as: "Buy Sony headphones under ₹25000"
- explain that the AI first parses the intent
- show that it detects category, brand, budget, and confidence
- show product ranking results
- explain why the system selected a product based on rating, availability, price, and fit

## 4. Show policy enforcement (45 seconds)
- explain the spending limits and approval thresholds
- show what happens when the request exceeds policy
- mention that the workflow blocks unsafe purchases and requests human approval when needed

## 5. Show approval flow (45 seconds)
- trigger a higher-value purchase or approval-needed scenario
- explain that approval is required before continuing
- show the approver action and the resulting flow

## 6. Show payment flow (45 seconds)
- continue the approved purchase
- explain that a Razorpay payment order is created
- show how the transaction stays recorded for audit purposes

## 7. Close (20 seconds)
"This project demonstrates the combination of AI, policy enforcement, and payment orchestration into one business workflow. It is relevant for procurement, employee spend management, and AI-assisted commerce."

## Suggested talk track
The best way to describe the project is:
"We built an AI buyer agent that acts like a smart procurement assistant. It reduces search time, enforces spending policies, routes approvals when needed, and ensures every purchase is auditable and payment-ready."

## Strongest project narrative
- AI understands demand in natural language
- product selection is ranked automatically
- compliance is enforced before payment
- approval gates handle exceptions
- payment and transaction records create trust and traceability

This makes the project feel like a real business workflow, not just an app with a search box.
